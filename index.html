About the app

Nufiala brings PDF textbooks, teacher manuals, learner guides, past questions and study guides together in one lightweight app, aligned with the GES/NaCCA curriculum and WASSCE preparation.

Built for Ghana

The app is designed to run well on budget Android devices and limited data connections.
